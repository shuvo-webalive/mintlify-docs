A purchase order records what you are buying from a supplier. These endpoints create, update, change, cancel and reactivate purchase orders, read their lines, KPIs, purchase invoices and payments, and manage their notes and attached files. There are 23 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **Deleting a purchase order, a note or a note file cannot be undone.** Cancel a purchase order instead if you may need it again.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Every write body is wrapped in `purchase_order`.** The one exception is the purchase invoice create, which is wrapped in `purchase_invoice`; the cancel and reactivate calls take an empty object.
- **The note calls take the purchase order's `code`, not its `id`.** That covers listing, creating, reading and deleting notes and their files.
- **A new purchase order is `active`.** Send `"draft": true` in the create body to save it as a draft instead.
- **Cancelling sets the status to `inactive`.** Reactivating sets it back to `active`.
- **Listings page by cursor.** Records are under `data`; pass `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor` while `pagination.has_more` is `true`.
- **Notes are created as a multipart form.** The text goes in the `note` field, and a file is optional.
