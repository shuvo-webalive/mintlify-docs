Workflows define the stages and milestones a record moves through. Use these endpoints to create and change workflows and to read their stages, milestones and the workflow history of custom component instances. This area has 15 endpoints, and all seven Exsited SDKs call every one.

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Creates and updates are wrapped in `workflow`.** One workflow comes back under `workflow` too, and `name` is required to create one.
- **A new workflow starts as a draft.** An update can move a draft to active, and move an active workflow to inactive and back.
- **A workflow is read by its `wf_` id or its `code`.** The milestone listing takes the `wf_` id in its `workflow_uuid` slot.
- **Component instance routes take the component's dashed uuid.** Under `/workflows/custom_components`, `component_uuid` is the hex of the `cmp_` id with the dashes put back and `object_code` is the instance's `code`; the `cmp_` id and the bare hex return `404`.
- **Listings page by cursor.** Records sit under `data`; pass `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor` to read the next page, and stop when `pagination.has_more` is `false`.
