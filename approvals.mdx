Approvals hold an action until someone signs it off. You can list account and sale order approvals, read one account approval, and approve or reject a pending one. This area has 5 endpoints, and all seven Exsited SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **Approving or rejecting takes effect at once.** An approve runs the held action, and both send notifications.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Approve and reject name the record in the body.** Send `{"approval": {"entity", "resource_id", "note"}}`; the same two routes serve all 16 entities.
- **A decision takes effect at once.** Approving runs the held action, and both decisions send notifications.
- **The sale order listing path is camel case.** It is `/approvals/saleOrder`, not `sale_order`.
- **Listings page by cursor.** Records sit under `data`; pass `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor` to read the next page, and stop when `pagination.has_more` is `false`.
