Exsited puts your operational data to work through custom dashboards and a flexible report builder. Every dashboard is role-specific — your warehouse team sees stock and fulfilment metrics, your finance team sees invoice and payment KPIs, and your management team sees the big picture across all operations. Reports pull live data, support granular filtering, and export in the formats your team already uses.

## Custom Dashboards

Each role in Exsited can have its own personalised dashboard. Dashboards are built by dragging and dropping KPI tiles and charts onto a canvas — no code or configuration files required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a dashboard">
    Go to **Dashboards** and click **New Dashboard**. Give it a name and assign it to a role (or keep it personal).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add tiles">
    Click **Add Tile** and choose from the tile library — KPI numbers, bar charts, line charts, pie charts, tables, or activity feeds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure each tile">
    Click a tile to open its settings. Select the data source (orders, invoices, stock, approvals, etc.), the metric, the date range, and any filters. Preview updates in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange the layout">
    Drag tiles to reorder them and resize them by dragging their edges. Organise related tiles into sections with optional headings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Save & Publish**. Users with the assigned role see the dashboard the next time they log in or refresh their page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use **Duplicate Dashboard** to create a variant of an existing dashboard for a different role — then adjust the tiles rather than building from scratch.
</Tip>

## KPI Tiles

KPI tiles are the building blocks of your dashboards. Each tile displays a single metric with context — current value, comparison to a prior period, and a trend indicator.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stock levels" icon="boxes-stacked">
    Total stock on hand, low-stock SKU count, stock value by location or category, and days of inventory remaining.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Order flow" icon="cart-shopping">
    Orders by status, average fulfilment time, orders overdue, and new orders in a selected period.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Approval metrics" icon="clipboard-check">
    Pending approvals, average approval time, rejection rate, and submissions by portal or form.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Finance KPIs" icon="chart-line">
    Revenue, outstanding invoices, overdue amounts, payments received, and gross margin.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Every KPI tile supports a **date range selector** so users can switch between today, this week, this month, this quarter, or a custom range without leaving the dashboard.

## Report Builder

The report builder lets you create fully custom tabular reports by selecting a data source, adding columns, applying filters, and grouping results.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report builder">
    Go to **Reports → Report Builder** and click **New Report**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a data source">
    Choose the primary data source for your report — Orders, Invoices, Payments, Purchase Orders, Stock, Portal Submissions, or Customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add columns">
    Drag columns from the field panel on the left onto the report canvas. Reorder columns by dragging their headers. Set column width and display format (e.g. currency, date, percentage).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Click **Add Filter** to restrict results. Filter by date range, account, status, category, custom field, or any column in your report. Combine multiple filters with AND / OR logic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Group and summarise">
    Use **Group By** to aggregate rows (e.g. group orders by customer, or invoices by month). Add summary rows for totals, averages, or counts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and share">
    Save the report. Assign it to a role or share it with specific users. Saved reports appear in the **Reports** menu for quick access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Reports always pull live data — every time you run a saved report, you see the current state of your data at that moment, not a cached snapshot.
</Note>

Exsited ships with a library of pre-built reports covering the most common operational and financial needs. You can run them as-is or use them as starting points to build custom variants.

<Accordion title="Available built-in reports">

## Export Options

Every report and dashboard tile supports export. Run a report, then click **Export** to choose your format.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="CSV export" icon="file-csv">
    Export the full report data as a comma-separated values file. Compatible with Excel, Google Sheets, and any data tool.
  </Card>
  <Card title="PDF export" icon="file-pdf">
    Export a formatted PDF version of the report, including your account name, report title, date range, and applied filters in the header.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Schedule a report to run automatically and deliver by email at a set frequency (daily, weekly, or monthly). Configure scheduled reports under **Reports → Scheduled Reports → New Schedule**.
</Tip>

## Audit Metadata

Every report includes an **audit metadata** section at the top of the exported document and in the report viewer:

- The user who ran the report
- The date and time the report was generated
- The filters and date range applied
- The data source and report version

This metadata ensures that exported reports can be traced back to their exact parameters — useful for compliance, reconciliation, and sharing with external auditors.

<Info>
  Audit metadata is always included in PDF exports and appears as a hidden sheet in CSV exports when opened in Excel, so it does not interfere with data processing.
</Info>

## Role-Based Dashboard Assignment

Dashboards can be assigned to roles so that users always land on a dashboard relevant to their responsibilities.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assign a default dashboard to a role">
    Go to **Settings → Roles → [Role Name]** and select a dashboard under **Default Dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow personal dashboards">
    Toggle **Allow Personal Dashboards** if you want users in this role to also be able to create and customise their own dashboards in addition to the default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Users can always switch between dashboards they have access to using the dashboard selector in the top navigation. The default dashboard is what they see on first login each session.
</Note>
