Exsited gives you a complete billing workflow in one place. You can raise invoices manually, have them generated automatically when an order is completed, record payments against them, issue credit notes for adjustments, and process refunds — all with multi-currency and GST/VAT support built in. Every transaction syncs in real time to your connected accounting system so your books are always up to date.

## Creating Invoices

Invoices can be created in two ways: automatically from a completed order, or manually for standalone billing scenarios.

### Auto-Generated from Orders

When an order reaches **Completed** status, Exsited automatically creates an invoice linked to that order. The invoice inherits all line items, prices, tax, shipping, and customer details from the order. No manual entry is required.

<Note>
  You can configure whether invoices are created at order confirmation or at completion under **Settings → Invoices → Auto-Invoice Trigger**.
</Note>

### Manual Invoices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Invoices">
    Go to **Invoices & Payments → Invoices** and click **New Invoice**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a customer">
    Search for an existing customer account. Billing address, currency, and payment terms pre-fill from the customer record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add line items">
    Add products, services, or free-text lines. Set the quantity, unit price, and tax class for each line. Exsited calculates the line total and invoice total in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set payment terms and due date">
    Choose the payment terms (e.g. Net 14, Net 30, Due on Receipt). The due date calculates automatically from the invoice date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue the invoice">
    Click **Issue Invoice** to move it from Draft to Issued. You can download the PDF or send it directly to the customer by email from within Exsited.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use **Invoice Templates** under **Settings → Documents → Invoice** to configure your logo, colour scheme, payment instructions, and footer text so every invoice reflects your brand.
</Tip>

## Invoice Statuses

<Accordion title="Invoice status definitions">
  | Status | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | Draft | Created but not yet sent to the customer. Editable. |
  | Issued | Sent to the customer. Awaiting payment. |
  | Partial | At least one payment has been recorded, but the balance is still outstanding. |
  | Paid | The full invoice amount has been received and recorded. |
  | Overdue | The due date has passed and the balance remains unpaid. |
  | Voided | Cancelled before payment. Cannot be re-opened; raise a new invoice if needed. |
  | Credit Applied | A credit note has been applied to reduce the invoice balance. |
</Accordion>

## Recording Payments

When a customer pays, record the payment against the invoice so Exsited and your accounting system stay accurate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invoice">
    Navigate to the invoice and click **Record Payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment details">
    Select the payment method (bank transfer, credit card, cash, or a custom method you have configured). Enter the amount received and the payment date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allocate the payment">
    If the customer is paying multiple invoices in a single transfer, you can allocate the payment across all relevant invoices from the same screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Payment**. The invoice status updates to **Paid** or **Partial** depending on whether the full amount was received. The payment syncs to your accounting system immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Payments processed through Exsited's PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway are recorded automatically — you do not need to enter them manually.
</Info>

## Credit Notes

Raise a credit note when you need to reduce an invoice balance — for returned goods, pricing errors, goodwill adjustments, or disputed charges.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the original invoice">
    Navigate to the issued or paid invoice and click **Actions → Raise Credit Note**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select lines and quantities">
    Choose the lines and quantities to credit. You can credit part of a line, a full line, or the entire invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reason">
    Enter a credit reason for audit and reporting purposes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue the credit note">
    Click **Issue Credit Note**. Exsited creates a linked credit note document and either applies it to the original invoice balance or leaves it as an unapplied credit on the customer account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Credit notes sync to your accounting system as credit notes in Xero, MYOB, or QuickBooks, maintaining a complete and reconcilable audit trail.
</Note>

## Refunds

When a credit note results in a cash refund rather than a balance adjustment, process the refund from the credit note record.

- Open the credit note and click **Process Refund**.
- Select the refund method (original payment method, bank transfer, or manual).
- Enter the refund amount and date.
- Click **Confirm Refund**. The refund is recorded in Exsited and synced to your accounting system.

<Warning>
  Refunds processed through the Exsited payment gateway are subject to your payment provider's processing timeframes. The status updates in Exsited automatically when the refund is confirmed by the gateway.
</Warning>

## Multi-Currency Support

Exsited supports invoicing in any currency. When you create an invoice for a customer whose account currency differs from your base currency, Exsited:

- Displays the invoice in the customer's currency.
- Converts the amount to your base currency using the exchange rate you specify (or the live rate if you have currency auto-update enabled).
- Records both the foreign currency amount and the base currency equivalent in the invoice and in your accounting system.

Exchange rates are configured under **Settings → Currencies**. You can set a fixed rate per currency pair or enable live rate updates from a connected exchange rate provider.

## GST and VAT Handling

Exsited calculates GST and VAT automatically based on tax classes assigned to your products and the tax rules configured for your account.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Tax classes" icon="percent">
    Assign a tax class to every product (e.g. Standard Rate, Zero Rate, Exempt). Exsited applies the correct rate to each line when generating an invoice.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tax rules by region" icon="globe">
    Configure different tax rates for different countries or states. Exsited applies the correct rate based on the customer's billing address.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tax-inclusive vs exclusive pricing" icon="calculator">
    Set whether your prices are entered tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive per price list. Exsited displays and prints the correct breakdown on every document.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tax reports" icon="chart-bar">
    Run a GST/VAT summary report for any date range from **Reports → Tax**. Export to CSV or PDF for your accountant or tax authority.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Audit Trail

Every action on an invoice — creation, edit, payment, credit, or sync — is recorded in the invoice's **Activity Log**.

<Accordion title="What the audit trail captures">
  | Event | Detail recorded |
  |---|---|
  | Invoice created | User, date, source (manual or order) |
  | Invoice issued | User, date, delivery method |
  | Payment recorded | Amount, method, date, user |
  | Credit note raised | Lines credited, reason, user |
  | Refund processed | Amount, method, date |
  | Status change | From → To, timestamp, user |
  | Accounting sync | System, result, timestamp |
</Accordion>

The audit trail is immutable — it cannot be edited or deleted — giving you a complete, trustworthy record for compliance and dispute resolution.

## Sync to Xero, MYOB, and QuickBooks

All invoices, payments, credit notes, and refunds sync automatically to your connected accounting system.

- **Xero** — invoices sync as approved or draft invoices; payments create payment records against the invoice; credit notes sync as Xero credit notes.
- **MYOB** — invoices and payments sync to AccountRight or Essentials depending on your connection type.
- **QuickBooks** — invoices map to QuickBooks invoices with payment and credit records linked.

<Note>
  Sync settings, account code mappings, and tax code mappings are managed under **Settings → Integrations → Accounting**. Changes to mappings apply to new syncs only — previously synced records are not retroactively updated.
</Note>
