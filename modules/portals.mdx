Portals are secure, branded web experiences you deploy to clients, partners, or internal teams without writing a line of code. Each portal is fully configured inside Exsited using a drag-and-drop builder, and every form submission, approval step, and generated document is stored against the relevant account record. Use portals for client onboarding, compliance data collection, partner order submissions, internal approval workflows, or any other process that today lives in email threads or spreadsheets.

## What Portals Are

A portal is a role-aware web interface hosted by Exsited and accessible via a unique URL or embedded in your own website. Portals can be:

- **Client portals** — let customers submit requests, approve quotes, upload documents, or view their account activity.
- **Partner portals** — give distributors, resellers, or contractors their own workspace to place orders, check stock, and track fulfilment.
- **Staff portals** — create internal approval flows, compliance checklists, or data-entry screens for your operations team.

Each portal is independent with its own branding, access rules, and form configuration. A single Exsited account can run multiple portals simultaneously.

## Building a Portal with the Drag-and-Drop Form Builder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new portal">
    Go to **Portals → All Portals** and click **New Portal**. Give it a name, select a base colour scheme, and upload your logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the form builder">
    Click **Edit Forms** to open the drag-and-drop builder. The canvas shows a blank form page; the left panel shows available field types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add fields">
    Drag fields from the panel onto the canvas. Arrange them by dragging handles. Use sections and column layouts to organise related fields visually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure each field">
    Click any field to open its settings panel. Set the label, placeholder text, help text, validation rules (required, min/max, regex), and whether the field is visible to all roles or restricted to specific roles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add pages">
    Split long forms across multiple pages using the **Add Page** button. Users navigate between pages with Next / Back controls. Completion percentage appears in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and publish">
    Click **Preview** to see the form as a client or manager would. When satisfied, click **Publish**. The portal URL becomes active immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the **Mobile Preview** toggle in the builder to check how your form renders on small screens. Exsited forms are fully responsive by default.
</Tip>

### Conditional Logic

Show or hide fields, sections, and pages based on the values a user has already entered. To add conditional logic:

1. Click the field or section you want to control and open **Conditional Logic**.
2. Define the condition (e.g. *If "Business Type" equals "Wholesale"*).
3. Set the action: **Show**, **Hide**, **Require**, or **Set Value**.

Conditions can be combined with AND / OR operators. Conditional logic runs in the browser so the experience is instant — no page reloads.

## Multi-Role Access

Every portal user is assigned a role that controls what they see and what they can do.

<Accordion title="Role capabilities">
  | Role | Can submit forms | Can view all submissions | Can approve | Can manage portal settings |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Client | ✓ Own submissions only | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |
  | Manager | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
  | Admin | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
</Accordion>

You assign roles when you invite users to a portal. Roles can also be scoped to specific sub-accounts — see **Sub-Account Visibility** below.

To invite a user, go to **Portals → [Your Portal] → Users** and click **Invite User**. Enter their email address and select their role. They receive an invitation email with a secure link to set their password.

## Approval Workflows

For processes that require sign-off before action is taken, configure a multi-step approval workflow on any portal form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open workflow settings">
    In your portal, go to **Form Settings → Approval Workflow** and click **Add Workflow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define approval steps">
    Add one or more steps. For each step, specify:
    - The approver (a named user, a role group, or a dynamic rule based on form values)
    - Whether any one approver in the group can approve, or all must approve
    - The action on approval (advance to next step, trigger an action, notify the submitter)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define rejection behaviour">
    Set what happens if an approver rejects: return to submitter for revision, escalate to a senior approver, or close the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notifications">
    Configure email notifications at each step — to the approver, to the submitter, or to a team inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the workflow">
    Toggle the workflow on and publish the form. New submissions will enter the approval queue immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Approvers receive email notifications with a direct link to the submission. They can approve or reject from within the portal or from the notification email itself without needing to log in separately.
</Info>

## PDF Generation

When a form is submitted or an approval step is completed, Exsited can generate a formatted PDF document automatically.

- Go to **Form Settings → PDF Output** and enable PDF generation.
- Choose a layout template or build a custom one using the PDF designer. Drag field placeholders onto the document canvas to map form values to specific positions.
- Set the trigger: generate on submission, on approval, or both.
- The generated PDF is stored against the submission record and can be emailed to the submitter, the approver, or any fixed address automatically.

<Tip>
  Include your logo, signatory block, and a digital signature field so that approved PDFs are ready to send to clients or regulatory bodies without additional formatting.
</Tip>

## Sub-Account and Parent-Child Visibility

When your clients or partners have their own sub-accounts (e.g. a franchisor with multiple franchisees, or a wholesaler with multiple retail buyers), you can configure parent-child visibility so each account sees only its own data — while managers at the parent level see everything.

- Go to **Portals → [Your Portal] → Account Hierarchy** and map your account structure.
- Assign users to accounts. A user at a child account sees only submissions belonging to their account.
- A user with a **Manager** role at a parent account can view, filter, and act on submissions from all child accounts in their hierarchy.

<Note>
  Sub-account visibility applies to all portal forms, submission lists, and approval queues. It cannot be overridden at the individual user level — it is always enforced by account hierarchy.
</Note>

## Embedded Actions

Portal form submissions can trigger actions in Exsited automatically — removing the need for staff to manually process incoming requests.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Create records" icon="plus">
    Automatically create a customer, order, purchase request, or any custom record from form data when a submission is received or approved.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trigger sync" icon="rotate">
    Push the submitted data to a connected system — Xero, Shopify, or any integration — as soon as it is received.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Notify staff" icon="bell">
    Send an email or in-app notification to the relevant team or individual when a form is submitted or reaches a specific approval stage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Update existing records" icon="pen-to-square">
    Map form fields to update fields on an existing customer or order record, keeping your operational data current without manual data entry.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Configure embedded actions under **Form Settings → Actions**. Each action supports conditions so you can trigger different actions based on the form values or the approval outcome.

## Use Cases

<Accordion title="Client onboarding">
  Collect trading terms acknowledgement, ABN/business registration details, contact information, and signed agreements in a single branded flow. Auto-create the customer record and notify the account manager on completion.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Compliance and audits">
  Run periodic compliance check-ins where clients or staff submit supporting documents and certifications. Approval workflows ensure the right people review and sign off each submission. PDFs are generated and stored for audit purposes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Partner portals">
  Give distributors or resellers a dedicated portal to place orders, check product availability, and download price lists. Submitted orders can automatically create draft orders in Exsited for your fulfilment team to process.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Internal approval flows">
  Route purchasing requests, expense approvals, or policy exceptions through a defined approval chain without email. Each request is tracked, timed, and stored with a full activity log.
</Accordion>
