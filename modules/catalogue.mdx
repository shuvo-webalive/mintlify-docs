The Product Catalogue is the single source of truth for every item you sell. From simple SKUs to complex variant matrices and bundled products, you define your catalogue once in Exsited and it flows automatically to every connected sales channel — no manual re-entry, no inconsistencies.

## Creating Products and SKUs

Every item in your catalogue starts as a product record. A product holds the core identity (name, description, category, images) while one or more SKUs define the specific, stockable versions of that product.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Catalogue">
    Navigate to **Catalogue → Products** and click **New Product**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in product details">
    Enter the product name, description, category, and any images. These details appear on connected storefronts and in generated documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your base SKU">
    Under the **SKUs** tab, click **Add SKU**. Enter a unique SKU code, unit cost, retail price, and tax class. Exsited validates for duplicate codes across your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and publish">
    Click **Save**. The product is now active in your catalogue and ready to be pushed to connected channels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a consistent naming convention for SKU codes (for example, `TSHIRT-BLU-M`) so they stay readable in orders, purchase orders, and reports.
</Tip>

## Variants

When a product comes in multiple options — size, colour, material, or any other attribute — use variants instead of creating separate products. Variants share a parent product record but carry their own SKU, price, cost, stock level, and barcode.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable variants on a product">
    Open the product and toggle **Has Variants** on. Exsited will prompt you to define your variant attributes (for example, *Size* and *Colour*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define attribute options">
    Add the values for each attribute — for example, Size: S, M, L, XL and Colour: Black, White, Navy. Exsited generates a variant matrix automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit each variant">
    Review each generated variant row. Update SKU codes, prices, costs, and stock quantities as needed. You can bulk-edit rows to apply the same value to multiple variants at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each variant is treated as an independent SKU for stock, purchasing, and fulfilment purposes. The parent product acts as a display wrapper only.
</Note>

## Bundles

Bundles let you sell a curated set of SKUs as a single purchasable item — for example, a starter kit containing three individual products. Stock is deducted at the component level when a bundle is fulfilled.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a bundle product">
    Click **New Product** and select **Bundle** as the product type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add component SKUs">
    Use the **Bundle Components** panel to search for and add the individual SKUs that make up the bundle, along with the quantity of each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the bundle price">
    Enter the bundle's selling price. Exsited displays the sum of component costs alongside it so you can track your margin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  When an order containing a bundle is fulfilled, Exsited automatically deducts stock from each component SKU. The bundle itself does not hold stock.
</Info>

## Barcode Assignment

Barcodes connect your physical inventory to your digital catalogue. Exsited supports EAN-13, UPC-A, Code 128, and QR formats.

- To assign a barcode manually, open a SKU and enter the value in the **Barcode** field.
- To generate a barcode, click **Generate** next to the field — Exsited creates a unique Code 128 value and previews it immediately.
- Barcodes sync to Shopify and Square so that scanning at point-of-sale resolves to the correct SKU automatically.

## Custom Fields

Every product and SKU record supports custom fields so you can capture data specific to your business — certifications, lead times, supplier part numbers, hazard ratings, or anything else.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define custom fields">
    Go to **Settings → Custom Fields → Products** and click **Add Field**. Choose a field type (text, number, date, dropdown, checkbox, or file upload) and give it a label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill values on product records">
    Once defined, the field appears on every product edit screen under the **Custom Fields** tab. Fill in the value for each relevant product or SKU.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Supported custom field types">
  | Type | Use case |
  |---|---|
  | Text | Part numbers, notes, certifications |
  | Number | Lead time in days, minimum order quantity |
  | Date | Expiry date, launch date |
  | Dropdown | Status, category overrides |
  | Checkbox | Hazardous, fragile, requires refrigeration |
  | File upload | Safety data sheets, compliance documents |
</Accordion>

## Multi-Channel Visibility

Exsited connects your catalogue to Shopify, Amazon, Square, and BigCommerce. Once a channel integration is active, you control which products are visible on each channel directly from the product record.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Shopify" icon="shopify">
    Push products, variants, prices, and images to your Shopify storefront. Inventory levels stay in sync bidirectionally.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Amazon" icon="amazon">
    Map your SKUs to Amazon ASINs and sync prices, stock availability, and product details to your Seller Central listings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Square" icon="square">
    Sync your catalogue and real-time stock to Square POS so in-store sales are reflected immediately in Exsited.
  </Card>
  <Card title="BigCommerce" icon="store">
    Publish products to your BigCommerce storefront with full variant and pricing support.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

To control channel visibility, open any product, go to the **Channels** tab, and toggle on the channels you want to publish to.

## Real-Time Sync Behaviour

Exsited uses event-driven sync — changes do not wait for a scheduled batch. When you update a product name, price, image, or stock level, Exsited pushes the change to connected channels within seconds.

<Note>
  Changes you save to a product or SKU sync immediately to all active channels. If a channel is temporarily unavailable, Exsited queues the update and retries automatically — you do not need to manually re-push.
</Note>

Sync status is visible per channel on the product's **Channels** tab. A green indicator confirms the last successful push; a yellow indicator means a sync is pending; red indicates an error with a description you can act on.
