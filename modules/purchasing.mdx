Purchasing in Exsited covers everything from the moment you decide to restock a product to the moment the goods arrive in your warehouse and the supplier invoice is matched and paid. You can raise purchase orders manually, let Exsited trigger them automatically when stock hits a reorder point, and receive goods at multiple locations — all with a clear audit trail that feeds directly into your accounting system.

## Creating a Purchase Order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Purchasing">
    Go to **Purchasing → Purchase Orders** and click **New Purchase Order**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a supplier">
    Search for an existing supplier or create one inline. The supplier's default currency, payment terms, and delivery address pre-fill automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add line items">
    Search for the SKUs you want to order. Exsited shows the current stock on hand and the configured reorder quantity for each item. Adjust quantities and unit costs per line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set delivery details">
    Choose the receiving location, expected delivery date, and any supplier reference numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or save">
    Save as a draft to review later, or click **Send to Supplier** to mark it as issued and generate a PDF copy for the supplier.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use **Actions → Duplicate PO** to quickly create a repeat order from a previous purchase order. All line items and costs carry over and you can adjust quantities before issuing.
</Tip>

## Reorder Triggers

Rather than monitoring stock levels manually, you can configure reorder points on any SKU and let Exsited generate purchase order suggestions automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set reorder parameters on a SKU">
    Open the SKU in **Catalogue → Products**, go to the **Purchasing** tab, and enter:
    - **Reorder Point** — the stock level at which a PO suggestion is created
    - **Reorder Quantity** — how much to order when the trigger fires
    - **Preferred Supplier** — the supplier to use on the generated PO
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review suggestions">
    When any SKU's available stock falls to or below its reorder point, Exsited creates a draft PO suggestion visible in **Purchasing → Reorder Suggestions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or edit">
    Review the suggestion, adjust quantities if needed, and click **Convert to PO** to create the purchase order. You can consolidate multiple suggestions for the same supplier into a single PO.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Reorder suggestions are recalculated in real time as sales and fulfilment events occur. Exsited will not create duplicate suggestions for the same SKU if one is already pending approval.
</Note>

## Supplier Profiles

Each supplier you work with has a dedicated profile that stores everything you need to trade with them effectively.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Contact details" icon="address-book">
    Name, address, phone, email, and a primary contact person. Used to auto-populate purchase orders and supplier communications.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payment terms" icon="handshake">
    Default payment terms (e.g. Net 30), preferred payment method, and bank account details for EFT payments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Default currency" icon="coins">
    Set the currency for all orders with this supplier. Exsited converts costs to your base currency for reporting.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Linked SKUs" icon="boxes-stacked">
    View all SKUs that list this supplier as preferred or secondary, and see recent purchase history at a glance.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

To create or edit a supplier profile, go to **Purchasing → Suppliers** and click **New Supplier** or select an existing one.

## Multi-Location Receiving

When goods arrive, you record a receipt against the purchase order. Exsited supports receiving the same PO across multiple locations — useful for drop-ship scenarios or split deliveries to different warehouses.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the purchase order">
    Navigate to the issued PO and click **Receive Stock**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the receiving location">
    Choose which warehouse or store location is receiving this delivery. You can create a partial receipt limited to a subset of lines or quantities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter received quantities">
    For each line item, enter the quantity actually received. Exsited flags any discrepancy between the ordered and received quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the receipt">
    Click **Confirm Receipt**. Stock on hand at the chosen location is updated immediately, and the PO's outstanding quantity is reduced.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you receive a quantity greater than what was ordered, Exsited will ask you to confirm the over-receipt before updating stock. Review with your supplier before accepting unexpected quantities.
</Warning>

## Cost and Tracking per Line Item

Each line on a purchase order carries its own cost, tracking, and notes:

- **Unit cost** — the agreed price per unit for this order, which may differ from the SKU's default cost.
- **Line total** — calculated automatically from quantity × unit cost, with currency and tax treatment applied.
- **Supplier SKU / part number** — record the supplier's own item code for easier reconciliation.
- **Notes** — free-text notes per line for special instructions or discrepancy records.

When you confirm a receipt, the received cost per unit updates the SKU's **Last Cost** field, giving you an accurate landed-cost history over time.

<Accordion title="Cost fields explained">
  | Field | Description |
  |---|---|
  | Unit Cost | Price agreed with the supplier for this PO line |
  | Last Cost | The most recent received unit cost, updated on receipt |
  | Average Cost | Rolling weighted average across all received quantities |
  | Landed Cost | Unit cost plus any freight or duty allocation |
</Accordion>

## Syncing POs to Accounting Systems

Exsited syncs purchase orders and receipts to Xero, MYOB, and QuickBooks so your accounts payable stays in step with your purchasing activity.

- When a PO is **issued**, Exsited can optionally create a draft bill in your accounting system.
- When stock is **received**, the bill is updated with the actual received quantities and costs.
- When a **supplier invoice** is matched and approved in Exsited, the bill is approved and ready for payment in your accounting system.

<Note>
  Account code mappings for purchase lines are configured under **Settings → Integrations → Accounting → Purchase Mappings**. You can set default expense accounts per supplier or per product category.
</Note>

## Purchase Invoices and Payments

After receiving stock, you can match the supplier's invoice to the purchase order within Exsited.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the received PO">
    Go to the PO and click **Add Supplier Invoice**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter invoice details">
    Record the supplier's invoice number, invoice date, due date, and any adjustments for freight or charges not on the original PO.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match to receipt">
    Exsited auto-populates the invoice lines from the receipt. Review and confirm the match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve and sync">
    Approve the invoice to mark it payable. It syncs to your accounting system as an approved bill. Record the payment in Exsited or in your accounting platform — the status stays in sync either way.
  </Step>
</Steps>
