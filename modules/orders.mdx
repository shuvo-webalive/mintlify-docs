Order Management in Exsited gives you complete visibility and control over every sale, from the moment a draft is created to the point it is fully delivered and accounted for. Whether orders arrive from a connected storefront, are entered manually by your team, or are generated programmatically via the API, they all flow through the same unified lifecycle — with every status change, edit, and user action logged automatically.

## Order Lifecycle

Each order moves through a defined set of statuses. You can progress orders manually or let automations advance them based on fulfilment events.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    An order is saved but not yet confirmed. Line items, pricing, and shipping details can still be edited freely. Draft orders do not affect stock on hand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmed">
    The order has been reviewed and accepted. Stock is reserved against the order. A PDF order confirmation can be generated and sent to the customer at this stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="In Fulfilment">
    Picking, packing, or shipment preparation is underway. Partial fulfilment is supported — see below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Completed">
    All line items have been fulfilled and dispatched. The order is closed and synced to your accounting system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancelled">
    The order was voided before completion. Reserved stock is released. A cancellation reason is recorded in the activity log.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can reopen a completed order to raise a credit note or process a return. See [Invoices & Payments](/modules/invoices-payments) for details on credits and refunds.
</Note>

## Creating an Order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Orders">
    Go to **Orders → All Orders** and click **New Order**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a customer">
    Search for an existing customer account or create a new one inline. Billing and shipping addresses pre-fill from the customer record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add line items">
    Search for products or SKUs and add them to the order. Exsited shows real-time stock availability next to each item. Adjust quantities, unit prices, and discounts per line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply shipping and taxes">
    Enter shipping details and select the applicable tax class. Exsited calculates GST/VAT automatically based on your tax settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the order">
    Review the order summary and click **Confirm Order**. Stock is reserved and the order moves to the Confirmed status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the **Quick Order** shortcut (keyboard shortcut `N` on the Orders list) to create an order without leaving your current view.
</Tip>

## Split Orders

When different line items need to ship from different locations — or at different times — you can split a single order into two or more fulfilment orders without losing the parent order record.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the order">
    Navigate to the confirmed order and click **Actions → Split Order**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign lines to splits">
    Drag line items into the split groups, or use the checkbox selectors to assign lines. Each split can have its own shipping address, carrier, and fulfilment location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the split">
    Click **Confirm Split**. Exsited creates child fulfilment orders linked back to the parent. The parent order reflects the combined status of all children.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Customers receive a single order reference regardless of how many splits are created. Tracking information for each shipment is appended to the same order record.
</Info>

## Partial Fulfilment

You do not need to wait for all items to be available before dispatching what you have. Exsited supports partial fulfilment at the line-item level.

- Open an order in **In Fulfilment** status.
- Click **Fulfil Items** and enter the quantities you are dispatching now. Leave remaining quantities for a later fulfilment run.
- Exsited records the partial fulfilment event with a timestamp and user stamp, and updates the outstanding quantity on each line.
- Repeat the process when the remaining stock is ready to ship.

<Warning>
  Marking all lines as fully fulfilled moves the order to **Completed** and triggers the accounting sync. Ensure all details are correct before completing the final fulfilment.
</Warning>

## PDF Order Confirmations

Generate a branded PDF confirmation for any confirmed or in-fulfilment order.

- Open the order and click **Actions → Generate PDF Confirmation**.
- Exsited uses your account's PDF template (logo, colours, footer) to produce the document.
- Download it directly or send it to the customer's email address from within the order screen.

<Tip>
  Customise your PDF template under **Settings → Documents → Order Confirmation**. You can add custom fields, adjust column layout, and include your terms and conditions.
</Tip>

## Custom Fields on Orders

Capture business-specific data on every order using custom fields — for example, a purchase order number provided by the customer, a project code, a sales rep name, or a delivery instruction flag.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define order custom fields">
    Go to **Settings → Custom Fields → Orders** and add the fields you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill values on each order">
    The fields appear on the order edit screen under **Custom Fields**. They are also available as columns in the Orders list view and as filter criteria in reports.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Syncing Orders to Accounting

Exsited connects to Xero, MYOB, and QuickBooks. When an order reaches **Completed** status, Exsited automatically creates the corresponding invoice in your accounting system.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Xero" icon="file-invoice-dollar">
    Orders sync as approved invoices in Xero, including line items, tax, and customer mapping.
  </Card>
  <Card title="MYOB" icon="file-invoice-dollar">
    Completed orders create sales invoices in MYOB AccountRight or Essentials, with full line-item detail.
  </Card>
  <Card title="QuickBooks" icon="file-invoice-dollar">
    Orders map to QuickBooks invoices, respecting your account mappings for income and tax codes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  If a customer does not yet exist in your accounting system, Exsited creates the contact record automatically on first sync. You can review and merge duplicates from **Settings → Integrations → Accounting**.
</Note>

## Status Timestamps and User Logs

Every status change and edit on an order is recorded in the **Activity Log**, visible at the bottom of each order record. The log captures:

- The previous and new status
- The timestamp (in your account timezone)
- The user who made the change
- Any note or reason attached to the action

<Accordion title="What the activity log records">
  | Event | Detail captured |
  |---|---|
  | Order created | User, channel source, creation time |
  | Status change | From → To status, user, timestamp |
  | Line item edit | Field changed, old value, new value |
  | Fulfilment event | Items dispatched, quantities, carrier |
  | PDF generated | Document type, user, timestamp |
  | Accounting sync | Target system, sync result, timestamp |
  | Cancellation | Reason, user, timestamp |
</Accordion>

The activity log is read-only and cannot be edited or deleted, providing a reliable audit trail for every order in your account.
