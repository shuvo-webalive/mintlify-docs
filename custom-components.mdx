A custom component is a record type you define, such as a service contract, whose objects belong to accounts and carry their own attributes, notes, activity log and workflow. This area has 39 endpoints covering component definitions, their instances and objects, and everything attached to an object, and all seven SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **Replacing an object's linked accounts or orders unlinks every record you leave out, and changing its workflow status runs the transition and its actions at once.**
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **`component_uuid` takes two kinds of value.** The instance calls on `/component/{component_uuid}` and `/component/{component_uuid}/{component_object_id}` with POST, PATCH and DELETE on an instance take the component's dashed `uuid`; every other call takes the definition's `cmp_` id.
- **Instance bodies use the component's name as their key.** `POST /component/{component_uuid}` and `PATCH /component/{component_uuid}/{component_object_id}` send and return the instance under the component's `name`, such as `service_contract`.
- **Object calls under `/components/` use `custom_component_object`.** Objects are created, read, updated and returned under that key, and `parents` names the record that owns the object.
- **Notes are sent as multipart forms, not JSON.** Put the text in the `note` field and any files beside it; a file's `uuid` in `attachments` is the `file_id` for the file calls.
- **A custom object instance is created on the custom object's own path.** The create has no `instances` segment and sends `custom_object_instances`; the read, update and delete use `instances/{instance_id}`, and the update sends `custom_object_instance`.
- **Listings page by cursor.** Pass `limit`, then the `next_cursor` from `pagination` as `cursor` while `has_more` is true.
