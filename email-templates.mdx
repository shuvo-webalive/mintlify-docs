Email templates are the shared settings records for outgoing email, each with a name and a subject. You can list, create, read, update and delete them. This area has 5 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [List email templates](/api-reference/email-templates/list-email-templates) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `…/email_templates` | Returns a page of email templates under `data`, with paging details under `pagination`. |
| [Create an email template](/api-reference/email-templates/create-an-email-template) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#2563eb"}}>POST</span> | `…/email_templates` | Creates an email template and returns it under `email_template`. |
| [Retrieve an email template](/api-reference/email-templates/retrieve-an-email-template) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `…/email_templates/{email_template_id}` | Returns one email template by its `id`, under `email_template`. |
| [Delete an email template](/api-reference/email-templates/delete-an-email-template) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#c2410c"}}>DELETE</span> | `…/email_templates/{email_template_id}` | Deletes an email template. ## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **`name` is required on a create.** Send it, and usually a `subject`, under `email_template`.
- **`cc` and `bcc` are lists of addresses.** Send each as an array of strings, not one comma separated string.
- **An email template `id` starts with `et_`.**
- **Listings page by cursor.** Pass `limit` for the page size, then pass `pagination.next_cursor` back as `cursor` while `pagination.has_more` is true.
- **A delete returns `204` with no body.** Reading the email template afterwards returns `404`.
