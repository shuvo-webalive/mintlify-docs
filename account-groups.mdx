Account groups sort your accounts into sets, such as wholesale or retail customers. Here you list, create, read, update and delete them. This area has 5 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [List account groups](/api-reference/account-groups/list-account-groups) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/settings/account_groups` | Lists your account groups, a page at a time. |
| [Create an account group](/api-reference/account-groups/create-an-account-group) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#2563eb"}}>POST</span> | `/settings/account_groups` | Creates an account group and returns it under `account_group`. |
| [Retrieve an account group](/api-reference/account-groups/retrieve-an-account-group) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/settings/account_groups/{account_group_id}` | Returns one account group, under `account_group`. |
| [Delete an account group](/api-reference/account-groups/delete-an-account-group) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#c2410c"}}>DELETE</span> | `/settings/account_groups/{account_group_id}` | Deletes an account group that no account uses. |
| [Update an account group](/api-reference/account-groups/update-an-account-group) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#7a5af8"}}>PATCH</span> | `/settings/account_groups/{account_group_id}` | Changes an account group's fields or its status. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **A group is named by its `ag_` id.** List account groups returns each group's `id`.
- **`name` is required to create a group.** The new group comes back under `account_group`.
- **One group comes back under `account_group`; a listing comes back under `data`.** A listing also carries `pagination`; pass `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor` while `has_more` is `true`.
- **Only a group no account uses can be deleted.**
- **Updating or deleting a group can fail with `500`.** It does so even for a group just created, so a group you create may not be removable through the API.
