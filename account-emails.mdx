An account keeps a history of the emails sent to or from it. This area deletes one email from that history; listing and reading emails are in Accounts. It has 1 endpoint, and all seven SDKs call it.

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Delete an account email](/api-reference/account-emails/delete-an-account-email) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#c2410c"}}>DELETE</span> | `…/emails/{email_uuid}` | Deletes one email from an account's email history. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **The email is named by its `uuid`.** List an account's emails, in Accounts, returns each email's `uuid`.
- **The account is named by its `id`.** The `id` is a short code such as `W13ZGY`, not the account's `uuid`.
- **A successful delete returns `200`.** Retrieve an email, in Accounts, then returns `404` for that `uuid`.
