A zone is a named group of countries. Shipping rules name zones in their configuration. These endpoints list, read, create, update and delete zones. This area has 5 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [List zones](/api-reference/zones/list-zones) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/settings/zones` | Returns a page of zones. |
| [Create a zone](/api-reference/zones/create-a-zone) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#2563eb"}}>POST</span> | `/settings/zones` | Creates a zone from a name and a list of countries, and returns it. |
| [Retrieve a zone](/api-reference/zones/retrieve-a-zone) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/settings/zones/{zone_id}` | Returns one zone by its `id`. |
| [Delete a zone](/api-reference/zones/delete-a-zone) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#c2410c"}}>DELETE</span> | `/settings/zones/{zone_id}` | Deletes a zone. | [Update a zone](/api-reference/zones/update-a-zone) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#7a5af8"}}>PATCH</span> | `/settings/zones/{zone_id}` | Changes a zone's details and returns the zone. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **A zone needs at least one country.** `country_list` takes country `id` values, the `country_` ids that `GET /settings/countries` returns.
- **Writes are wrapped in `zone`, and one zone comes back under `zone`.** A new zone's `display_name` is set from its `name`.
- **Zone ids are prefixed with `zn_`.** A shipping rule configuration names a zone by its dashed uuid instead, built from the 32 hex digits after `zn_`.
- **The listing pages by cursor.** Pass `limit`, then send the `next_cursor` from `pagination` as `cursor` until `has_more` is `false`.
