Express creates a complete order for an account in one request: the order, its lines and its invoice. This area has 1 endpoint, and all seven SDKs call it.

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Create an express order](/api-reference/express/create-an-express-order) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#2563eb"}}>POST</span> | `…/express` | Creates an active order for an existing account in one call and returns it under `express`. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **The body sits under `account`.** Name the account by `id` and nest the order under `account.order`.
- **The order is created active.** The new order's `status` is `active`, and one invoice is raised for it straight away.
- **The response sits under `express`.** It holds the order as `get_order` reads it, including `lines`, `kpis` and `billing_preferences`.
- **Each line names an item by `item_id`.** `item_price_snapshot.pricing_rule.price` sets the line's price, and `tax_exempt_when_sold` removes tax from the line.
