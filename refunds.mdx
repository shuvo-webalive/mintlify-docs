A refund pays money back to an account out of a credit note. This area has 12 endpoints: create a refund from a credit note, read, update and delete refunds, and manage their notes and files. Every SDK supports all of them.

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Identify a refund by its code.** `{refund_id}` is the code the refunds listing returns as `id`.
- **A refund comes from a credit note.** Create one under the credit note, identified by its `code`, with `amount` and `payment_method` under `refund`.
- **Amounts are strings.** A refund's `amount` reads like `"2.00"`.
- **Note deletes check the create permission.** Deleting a refund note or its file needs `CREATE_REFUND_NOTE`; without it the call returns `403`.
- **Listings page by cursor.** Records are under `data`. Pass `pagination.next_cursor` back as `cursor` while `pagination.has_more` is `true`.
