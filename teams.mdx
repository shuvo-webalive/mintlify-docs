Teams group people in your Exsited settings. You can list, create, retrieve, update and delete teams, and enable or disable one through its `status`. This area has 5 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **A team with members cannot be deleted, and a delete cannot be undone.** Remove the members first; after a delete, reading the team returns `404`.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Request bodies go under `team`.** Both the create and the update wrap their fields in a `team` object, and every single-team response returns it under `team`.
- **Team ids are not uuids.** An id such as `team_00000000000000010000000000000006` encodes the parent organisation and a row number.
- **`version` is always null on a team.** Do not rely on it for change detection.
- **`status` takes `active` or `inactive`.** Send it in an update to enable or disable a team.
- **`display_name` defaults to `name`.** A team created with only a `name` gets the same value as its `display_name`.
