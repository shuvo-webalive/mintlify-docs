System Settings is the control centre for your Exsited workspace. From here you can update your organisation's details, configure how taxes and currencies are handled, personalise the look of your portals, and control the notifications your team receives. Changes made in System Settings apply across your entire organisation, so take care to review each section before saving.

To access System Settings, navigate to **Configuration → System Settings** from the main navigation menu.

## Organisation Details

The Organisation Details section holds your core business information, which is used across documents, portals, and integrations.

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Organisation Name** | Your legal or trading business name. Appears on PDFs, portal headers, and email notifications. |
| **Business Address** | Your registered or primary operating address. Used on invoices and purchase orders. |
| **ABN / Tax Number** | Your Australian Business Number, VAT registration number, or equivalent tax identifier. |
| **Logo** | Upload your organisation's logo (PNG or SVG recommended). Displayed on portals, documents, and email templates. |
| **Brand Colours** | Set your primary and secondary brand colours using hex codes. Applied to portal headers, buttons, and accents. |

<Note>
  Logo files should be no larger than 2 MB. For best results, upload a version with a transparent background at a minimum of 300 × 100 px.
</Note>

## Currency Settings

Exsited supports a single base currency and optional multi-currency display for organisations that deal with international customers or suppliers.

- **Base Currency** — select your primary operating currency (e.g., AUD, USD, GBP). This is the currency used for all internal calculations, reports, and accounting integrations.
- **Multi-Currency Support** — when enabled, you can record transactions in foreign currencies. Exsited converts amounts to your base currency using exchange rates that you can set manually or sync from a connected accounting platform.
- **Exchange Rate Source** — choose between **Manual** (you update rates yourself) or **Accounting Platform** (rates are pulled automatically from your connected accounting integration).

<Note>
  If your connected accounting platform handles multi-currency natively (such as Xero or MYOB), Exsited will defer to that platform's exchange rate settings to avoid conflicts.
</Note>

## Tax Configuration

Configure the tax rules that apply to your transactions. Exsited supports GST, VAT, and custom tax codes to match your business's obligations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tax Settings">
    Go to **Configuration → System Settings → Tax**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your tax framework">
    Choose **GST** (for Australian businesses), **VAT** (for UK and European businesses), or **Custom** if your region uses a different structure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the default tax rate">
    Enter the standard rate as a percentage (e.g., 10 for 10% GST or 20 for 20% VAT).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add custom tax codes">
    Click **Add Tax Code** to define additional rates — for example, a reduced rate for specific goods, or a zero-rated export code. Give each code a short identifier (e.g., *GST-FREE*, *REDUCED*) that appears in dropdowns when creating records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the default tax code">
    Choose which tax code is pre-selected when creating new orders, invoices, or purchase orders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Tax Settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Branding

The Branding section lets you extend your visual identity across every customer-facing surface in Exsited.

- **Portal Theme** — select a light or dark base theme for your customer and supplier portals.
- **Primary Colour** — used for buttons, links, and highlights.
- **Secondary Colour** — used for secondary actions and backgrounds.
- **Font** — choose from a curated set of web-safe fonts for portal headings and body text.
- **Email Header** — customise the header image and colour shown on outbound email notifications.
- **Favicon** — upload a small icon (32 × 32 px ICO or PNG) for browser tabs.

<Tip>
  Apply your brand colours consistently across portals and email templates to give your customers a cohesive, professional experience.
</Tip>

## Notification Preferences

Control which events trigger email alerts and in-app notifications for your team.

| Event | Who is notified |
|---|---|
| **Approval Required** | The assigned approver for the record. |
| **Approval Decision** | The record's creator (approved or rejected). |
| **Sync Failure** | Admin users and the user who initiated the sync. |
| **Low Stock Alert** | Users with Inventory module access (configurable threshold). |
| **New Portal Submission** | Users assigned to the relevant portal. |
| **Integration Disconnected** | Admin users. |

To manage notification preferences, go to **Configuration → System Settings → Notifications**. You can enable or disable each event type globally, and individual users can further customise their own preferences from their profile settings.

## Audit Logs

Exsited maintains a complete audit log of all changes made within your workspace, giving you a clear record of who did what and when.

**What is logged:**

- Record creation, edits, and deletions
- Approval decisions (approved, rejected, escalated)
- User management changes (invitations, role updates, deactivations)
- System settings changes
- Integration connections and disconnections
- Login events and session activity

To access the audit log, navigate to **Configuration → System Settings → Audit Logs**. You can filter by **date range**, **user**, **module**, or **action type**, and export the filtered log as a CSV file.

<Note>
  Audit log records are retained for 12 months by default. If your organisation requires a longer retention period, contact [support@exsited.com](mailto:support@exsited.com) to discuss extended retention options.
</Note>
