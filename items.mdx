Items are the products and services you sell and buy. These endpoints create, read, update and delete items, set their sale, purchase and inventory sections, add stock to warehouses, and manage bundles, families, variations, packages and notes. This area has 41 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **Deleting an item removes it, and a later read returns `404`.** An item that an order references is refused with `409`, and retrying does not help.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Every item write is wrapped in `item`.** The one exception is the image upload, which sends `image_base64` at the top level of the body.
- **Address an item by its `id`, such as `ITEM-0001`.** Not by its `uuid`, and not by the integer `id` that the sale and purchase reads return.
- **`type` and `name` are the minimum to create an item.** Send `type` in upper case, such as `STANDARD`, `BUNDLE`, `FAMILY` or `VARIATION_BASED`; it comes back in lower case.
- **The item listing pages by cursor.** Pass `limit`, then send the `next_cursor` from `pagination` as `cursor`; `has_more` tells you when to stop. Filter with `filter_<field>`; a bare `filter` expression is refused with `400`, and `order_by` and `direction` are ignored.
- **The sale, purchase and inventories reads return a short item.** Each carries `id`, `name` and its one section, and the sale and purchase reads give `id` as an integer.
- **Item notes are sent as a multipart form.** The text goes in the `note` field, and files are optional extra parts.
