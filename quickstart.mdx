Getting up and running with Exsited is straightforward. This guide walks you through the essential setup steps — from logging in for the first time to creating your first product and order. You can complete the core setup in under fifteen minutes, and every step is reversible, so feel free to explore as you go.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your Exsited workspace">
    Navigate to your Exsited workspace URL and sign in with the credentials provided during account creation. If you have not yet created an account, contact the Exsited team at [support@exsited.com](mailto:support@exsited.com) or call **1300 253 181** to get set up.

    Once logged in, you land on the main dashboard. Take a moment to familiarise yourself with the left-hand navigation — this is where you access every module, from Orders to Integrations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure your organisation">
    Before you create any records, set up your organisation details so that documents, invoices, and portal pages display the correct information.

    Go to **Settings → Organisation** and fill in:

    - **Company name, logo, and contact details** — used on invoices, portals, and outbound emails.
    - **Default currency** — set the base currency for all transactions. You can enable additional currencies later.
    - **Tax settings** — configure your tax rate(s) and choose whether prices are entered inclusive or exclusive of tax.
    - **Financial year and date format** — align Exsited with your reporting calendar and regional preferences.

    Save your changes before moving on.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Add the people who will work in Exsited and assign them the appropriate access level. Go to **Settings → Users & Roles** and select **Invite User**.

    Exsited includes four built-in roles:

    | Role | What they can do |
    |------|-----------------|
    | **Admin** | Full access to all modules, settings, and integrations |
    | **Manager** | Create and edit records across all modules; cannot change system settings |
    | **Staff** | Create and update records within assigned modules |
    | **Viewer** | Read-only access to all permitted modules |

    Enter the user's email address, select their role, and send the invitation. They will receive an email prompting them to set a password and log in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your first integration">
    Exsited becomes most powerful when it is connected to the tools you already use. Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** and choose the platform you want to connect first.

    Popular starting points include:

    - **Xero, MYOB, or QuickBooks** — sync invoices, payments, and bills automatically.
    - **Shopify or BigCommerce** — import orders and push inventory updates in real time.
    - **HubSpot or Zoho** — link CRM contacts to Exsited accounts for a unified customer view.

    Each integration has a step-by-step connection wizard. Follow the prompts, authorise the connection in the third-party platform, and confirm the field mappings. Exsited will begin syncing data according to the schedule you configure.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first product and order">
    With your organisation configured and at least one integration connected, you are ready to create live records.

    **Add a product:**
    1. Go to **Catalogue → Items** and select **New Item**.
    2. Enter a product name, SKU, unit of measure, and pricing.
    3. Add variants (size, colour, etc.) if the product comes in multiple options.
    4. Save the item — it is now available to add to orders and will sync to any connected eCommerce or accounting platform.

    **Raise an order:**
    1. Go to **Orders → Sales Orders** and select **New Order**.
    2. Search for or create a customer account, then add line items from your catalogue.
    3. Set the order status to **Confirmed** when ready to fulfil.
    4. Exsited will update inventory, trigger any connected fulfilment integrations, and generate an invoice for accounting sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  Stuck at any point? The Exsited support team is ready to help. Email [support@exsited.com](mailto:support@exsited.com) or call **1300 253 181** during business hours. You can also explore the rest of this documentation to dive deeper into any module.
</Tip>
