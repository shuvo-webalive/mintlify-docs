Exsited is built around a modular architecture: each area of your business — customers, products, orders, purchasing, billing, and integrations — is represented by a dedicated module with its own records, workflows, and settings. The modules are designed to work together seamlessly, so data flows automatically from a sales order through to an invoice and into your accounting platform, without manual re-entry. Understanding how each concept fits together will help you configure Exsited to match the way your business actually operates.

<Note>
  Exsited's modular design means you activate only the modules your business needs. If you do not use purchase orders today, you can leave that module disabled and switch it on later — without disrupting the rest of your setup.
</Note>

## Accounts

An **Account** is the central record for any customer, client, or business you transact with. Each account holds:

- **Contact details** — primary contacts, email addresses, phone numbers, and physical/billing addresses.
- **Billing configuration** — default payment terms, currency, and tax treatment.
- **Linked records** — all orders, invoices, purchase history, and portal access associated with that account in one place.

Accounts can represent individual consumers, business clients, or internal departments. When you connect a CRM like HubSpot or Zoho, accounts sync bidirectionally so your sales and operations teams always see the same customer data.

## Items / Catalogue

The **Item Catalogue** is your master product library. Every product, service, or component you buy or sell lives here as an **Item**. Key concepts within the catalogue include:

- **SKUs** — unique identifiers that track each distinct product across inventory, orders, and integrations.
- **Variants** — a single item can have multiple variants (e.g. size, colour, material), each with its own SKU, pricing, and stock level.
- **Bundles** — group multiple items into a single sellable unit. Exsited automatically manages component-level inventory when a bundle is sold.
- **Barcodes** — attach one or more barcodes to each item or variant for warehouse scanning and POS compatibility.

Changes to catalogue data propagate automatically to connected eCommerce platforms and accounting systems, keeping product listings and pricing consistent everywhere.

## Orders

A **Sales Order** represents a commitment from a customer to purchase goods or services. In Exsited, orders move through a defined lifecycle:

1. **Draft** — order is being built; no stock is reserved.
2. **Confirmed** — stock is allocated; fulfilment can begin.
3. **In Fulfilment** — picked, packed, or dispatched via a connected fulfilment integration (e.g. FedEx, Carton Cloud).
4. **Completed** — all line items fulfilled; invoice generated.
5. **Cancelled** — order voided; allocated stock released.

Each order carries line-item detail, customer reference, shipping address, and a full activity log. Orders trigger downstream actions automatically — inventory deductions, invoice creation, and accounting sync — based on the workflow rules you configure.

## Purchase Orders

A **Purchase Order (PO)** is a formal request to a supplier to deliver goods or services at an agreed price. In Exsited, the purchasing workflow follows these stages:

- **Draft PO** — built from reorder suggestions or manually; not yet sent to the supplier.
- **Sent** — PO transmitted to the supplier via email or integration.
- **Partially Received / Received** — stock is booked into inventory as it arrives against the open PO.
- **Billed** — supplier invoice matched to the PO and pushed to your accounting platform.

Receiving stock against a PO automatically updates on-hand quantities in the catalogue and triggers a supplier bill in Xero, MYOB, or QuickBooks if accounting sync is enabled.

## Invoices & Payments

**Invoices** in Exsited are generated automatically when a sales order is completed, or manually for standalone billing. Each invoice supports:

- **Partial payments** — record multiple payments against a single invoice as funds are received.
- **Credit notes** — issue credits against an invoice for returns, adjustments, or goodwill.
- **Refunds** — process full or partial refunds, with the transaction posted back to your accounting platform.

All billing activity syncs to your connected accounting system in real time, eliminating the need to re-enter transactions in Xero, MYOB, or QuickBooks.

## Portals

**Portals** are custom-branded web interfaces you build inside Exsited — no code required. You can use portals to:

- Give customers a self-service view of their orders, invoices, and account details.
- Provide staff with a simplified interface focused on a specific workflow (e.g. job completion forms for field technicians).
- Collect structured data via **custom forms** with conditional logic, file uploads, and automated routing.

Each portal is access-controlled: you decide which accounts, contacts, or user roles can log in and what data they can see or submit. Portal submissions can trigger order creation, status updates, or integration events automatically.

## Integrations

**Integrations** connect Exsited to the third-party platforms your business already relies on. Exsited supports two types:

- **Pre-built integrations** — configured through a wizard in **Settings → Integrations**. Available platforms include Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks, Shopify, BigCommerce, Amazon, eBay, HubSpot, Zoho, NetSuite, Square, Vend, FedEx, Carton Cloud, and AroFlo.
- **Custom integrations via API** — use the Exsited REST API (`/api/v3/{resource}`) with OAuth 2.0 authentication (`POST /api/v1/oauth2/token`) to build bespoke connections to any platform.

Each integration has its own field-mapping configuration, sync direction (one-way or bidirectional), and event triggers — giving you precise control over how data flows between Exsited and your other tools.

## Organisations

An **Organisation** is the top-level entity in Exsited — it represents your business (or one business unit within a larger group). All accounts, items, orders, and settings belong to an organisation. Exsited supports **multi-tenant** deployments, meaning a single Exsited environment can host multiple independent organisations, each with its own data, users, and integration configuration.

This makes Exsited well suited to:

- **Franchise groups** that need a shared platform but separate operational data per franchisee.
- **Managed service providers** administering Exsited on behalf of multiple client businesses.
- **Businesses with distinct trading entities** that need separate books but shared catalogue or reporting.

Users can be granted access to one or more organisations, with role permissions applied independently per organisation.
