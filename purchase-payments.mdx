A purchase payment records money paid to a supplier against purchase invoices. These endpoints record, update, delete and read purchase payments, list the purchase credit notes a payment raised, and manage payment notes and their files. There are 15 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **Deleting a purchase payment, a note or a note file cannot be undone.**
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Every write body is wrapped in `purchase_payment`.** Amounts are decimal strings such as `"1.00"`.
- **A payment needs `payment_method_applications`.** Each entry names a payment processor by `id` and an amount.
- **Paying more than the invoices take raises a purchase credit note.** List it with the purchase credit notes call on the payment.
- **The invoice scoped create pays the invoice in the path.** Send only `payment_method_applications` to it.
- **Listings page by cursor.** Records are under `data`; pass `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor` while `pagination.has_more` is `true`.
