An item receipt records items received against the lines of a purchase order. These endpoints create, list, read, update and delete receipts, list a purchase order's receipts, and delete a receipt's note. This area has 7 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **A completed item receipt cannot be deleted.** The delete is refused with `400` `stock_committed`.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Writes are wrapped in `item_receipt`.** A create names the `purchase_order`, a `receipt_status` such as `STARTED`, a `receipt_date`, and `lines`, each with a `purchase_order_line` id and a `receipt_quantity`.
- **Receipt ids start with `ir_`, and note ids with `note_`.** Pass them in the paths as they are.
- **The update changes a receipt's details.** It takes fields such as `tracking_number`, `receipt_note` and `receipt_date`, and raises the receipt's `version`.
- **No endpoint lists or creates a receipt's notes.** Only the note delete exists here.
- **The listing pages by cursor.** Pass `limit`, then send the `next_cursor` from `pagination` as `cursor` until `has_more` is `false`.
