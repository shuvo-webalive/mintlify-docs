A credit note holds credit owed to an account, raised against an invoice or by an overpayment, that can be applied to invoices or refunded. This area has 14 endpoints: create, read, update, apply and delete credit notes, read their balance items and applications, and manage their notes and files. All seven SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **Applying a credit note records a payment against each invoice it names, and the call returns the credit note rather than those payments.**
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Creating a credit note takes only `invoice_id` and `amount`.** Both go under `credit_note`, and any other key returns `400`. The amount is drawn from the invoice's active payments, oldest first.
- **Applying takes only `invoices`.** Each entry of `credit_note.invoices` takes `invoice_id` and `amount` and nothing else.
- **A credit note with an active refund cannot be deleted.** The delete is refused with `400` and the code `in_use`. It accepts the `cn_` id or the credit note's code.
- **Items and applications are not paged.** Both come back under `credit_note`, carrying only `object`, `href` and their own list.
- **Money comes back as text.** `initial_amount`, `refund_amount` and `remaining_amount` are strings.
