A shipping package describes a box by its length, width, height and weight, each with an optional unit. These endpoints list, read, create, update and delete shipping packages. This area has 5 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

## At a glance

| [List shipping packages](/api-reference/shipping-packages/list-shipping-packages) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `…/shipping_packages` | Returns a page of shipping packages. |
| [Create a shipping package](/api-reference/shipping-packages/create-a-shipping-package) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#2563eb"}}>POST</span> | `…/shipping_packages` | Creates a shipping package and returns it under `shipping_package`. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Writes are wrapped in `shipping_package`, and one package comes back under `shipping_package`.** A create can send just a `name` with some dimensions.
- **Dimensions are numbers.** `length`, `width`, `height` and `weight` take numbers such as `10` or `1.5`.
- **A `*_uom` field takes the unit's bare uuid.** Not the unit's prefixed `id` from `GET /settings/uom`.
- **The listing pages by cursor.** Pass `limit`, then send the `next_cursor` from `pagination` as `cursor` until `has_more` is `false`.
