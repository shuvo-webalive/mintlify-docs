Time periods define a length of time, such as one month, in your Exsited settings. You can list, create, retrieve, update and delete them, and enable or disable one through its `status`. This area has 5 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **Deleting a time period cannot be undone.** After a delete, reading the time period returns `404`.
</Warning>

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [List time periods](/api-reference/time-periods/list-time-periods) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `…/time_periods` | Lists the time periods set up in Exsited, one page at a time. |
| [Create a time period](/api-reference/time-periods/create-a-time-period) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#2563eb"}}>POST</span> | `…/time_periods` | Creates a time period, such as one month, and returns it under `time_period`. |
| [Retrieve a time period](/api-reference/time-periods/retrieve-a-time-period) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `…/time_periods/{time_period_id}` | Retrieves one time period by its `tp_` id. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Request bodies go under `time_period`.** Both the create and the update wrap their fields in a `time_period` object, and every single-record response returns it under `time_period`.
- **`period` goes in upper case and comes back in lower case.** Send `MONTHLY`; the response shows `monthly`.
- **Time period ids start with `tp_`.** Pass that `id` as `time_period_id`.
- **`status` takes `active` or `inactive`.** Send it in an update to enable or disable a time period.
