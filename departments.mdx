Departments are the organisational units configured in your Exsited settings, each tied to a division. You can list them and retrieve one by its id. This area has 2 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [List departments](/api-reference/departments/list-departments) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `…/departments` | Lists the departments set up in Exsited, one page at a time. |
| [Retrieve a department](/api-reference/departments/retrieve-a-department) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `…/departments/{department_id}` | Retrieves one department by its `dept_` id. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Departments are read only here.** This area lists and retrieves departments; it has no create, update or delete call.
- **Some listed departments have no id.** A department can come back with `id: null` and an `href` that ends in `/settings/departments/`, and it cannot be retrieved on its own. Pick one with an `id` to read it singly.
- **Department ids start with `dept_`.** Pass that `id` as `department_id`.
- **The list pages by cursor.** Records are under `data`; pass `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor` while `pagination.has_more` is `true`.
