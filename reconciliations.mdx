A reconciliation matches payments and refunds against a bank statement. This area has 2 endpoints, which list the payments and the refunds in one reconciliation. All seven SDKs call every one.

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [List a reconciliation's payments](/api-reference/reconciliations/list-a-reconciliations-payments) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/reconciliations/{reconciliation_id}/sale_payments` | Returns the payments included in a reconciliation. |
| [List a reconciliation's refunds](/api-reference/reconciliations/list-a-reconciliations-refunds) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/reconciliations/{reconciliation_id}/sale_refunds` | Returns the refunds included in a reconciliation. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **There is no endpoint for the reconciliation itself.** Only the two lists of its payments and refunds are available.
- **Name the reconciliation by its uuid or its numeric id.** Both forms are accepted in `{reconciliation_id}`.
- **An unknown reconciliation returns `404`.** The error code is `resource_not_found`.
- **Records are under `data`.** Both lists put their records under `data`.
