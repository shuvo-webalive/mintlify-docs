Employees are the staff records in Exsited, each with a code, contact details and custom attributes. You can list, create, retrieve, update and delete them. This area has 5 endpoints, each available in all seven SDKs.

<Warning>
  **Deleting an employee cannot be undone.** After a delete, reading the employee returns `404`.
</Warning>

## At a glance

| [Create an employee](/api-reference/employees/create-an-employee) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#2563eb"}}>POST</span> | `/employees` | Creates an employee and returns it under `employee`. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Request bodies go under `employee`.** Both the create and the update wrap their fields in an `employee` object, and every single-employee response returns it under `employee`.
- **An employee has both an `id` and a `code`.** The `id` starts with `emp_` and is what these calls take; the milestones-by-employee call takes the `code` instead.
- **A new employee needs only a `name`.** The response gives its `id`, `code` and the rest of the record.
- **The list pages by cursor.** Records are under `data`; pass `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor` while `pagination.has_more` is `true`.
