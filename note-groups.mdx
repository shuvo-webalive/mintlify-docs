Note groups gather notes and the users who share them. You can list, create, read, update and delete groups, and list a group's notes overall or as one user sees them. This area has 7 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Writes are wrapped in `note_group`.** One group comes back under the same key.
- **Users can be added at create time.** Send `note_users` with a `name` and an `email_address` for each.
- **`version` counts changes.** After the first update, `version` is 2.
- **A user's view of the notes takes a uuid or an email address.** A user the group does not hold returns `404`.
- **Listings page by cursor.** Records sit under `data`; pass `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor` to read the next page, and stop when `pagination.has_more` is `false`.
