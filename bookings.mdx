A booking is an order whose lines reserve a labour for set booking times. These endpoints create, preview, change and delete bookings, and read and update their lines, billing preferences, invoices, payments, fulfillments, custom objects and PDFs. The module has 33 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **Delete a booking removes whatever order has that `id`, of any type, and returns no body.** Check the `id` first: a read of it afterwards returns `404`.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **The `/booking/{booking_id}` reads, updates and delete work on any order.** They do not check the order's type. Change a booking and Preview a booking change accept only a booking-type order.
- **Each line of a new booking needs a labour and its booking times.** The rest of the body is the order body, under `order`: `account_id`, `name` and `lines`.
- **Most bodies sit under `order`; the change bodies sit under `booking`.** The line billing update takes `order.line.item_properties`, not `billing_preferences`.
- **Updates return the whole booking under `order`.** Single reads use their own keys: `order_line`, `custom_object`, `item_fulfillment`, `order_kpis` and `payment`.
- **Listings page by cursor.** Records are under `data`; pass `limit` and the previous page's `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor`, and stop when `has_more` is `false`.
- **There is no per-booking notes listing.** List booking notes returns the notes of every order, and `/booking/{booking_id}/notes` is refused.
