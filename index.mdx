Exsited is an integration-first operations platform designed for growing businesses. It brings together product catalogue management, order processing, purchasing, accounting sync, and custom portals — all connected to the tools you already use, from Shopify and Amazon to Xero, MYOB, and QuickBooks.

## Get Started in 3 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Account">
    Log in to your Exsited workspace and configure your organisation settings, user roles, and modules that match your business needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your Systems">
    Use prebuilt connectors to sync Exsited with your accounting platform (Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks), eCommerce channels (Shopify, Amazon), or CRM tools (HubSpot, Zoho).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Your Operations">
    Create products, process orders, manage purchase orders, issue invoices, and build custom portals — all from a single, unified platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>
