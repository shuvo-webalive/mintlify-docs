An invoice bills an account for items, either on its own or raised from an order. This area has 30 endpoints: create, read, amend, cancel, reactivate and delete invoices, and read their lines, KPIs, PDF links, notes, files, fulfillments, return merchandise authorisations and custom objects. All seven SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **`GET /invoices/{invoice_id}/lines/{line_id}` ignores `line_id` and always returns the invoice's first line.**
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Pass an invoice's `id`, never its `uuid`.** Every `{invoice_id}` is the invoice's `id`, such as `INV-0001`.
- **Write bodies go under an `invoice` key.** Create, update, amend, cancel and reactivate all wrap their fields in `invoice`. Amend, cancel, reactivate and creating an invoice for an order also work with no body.
- **Money comes back as text.** Amounts such as `total`, `subtotal` and `due` are strings like `"12.00"`. `discount_amount` and a line's tax `rate` are numbers.
- **The invoice listing pages by cursor.** Records are under `data`. Pass `pagination.next_cursor` back as `cursor` while `pagination.has_more` is `true`.
- **Only a single read carries the lines.** A listing entry has no `lines`; retrieve the invoice, or list its lines, to see them.
- **Some reads under an invoice do not check the invoice.** The credit note application read returns any application under any invoice that exists.
