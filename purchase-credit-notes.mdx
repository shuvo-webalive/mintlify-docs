A purchase credit note is credit a supplier owes you, raised when a payment exceeds its invoices or created against a purchase invoice. These endpoints create, read, update, apply and delete purchase credit notes and add or remove their notes. This area has 8 endpoints, each available in all seven SDKs.

<Warning>
  **Creating a purchase credit note cannot be reversed.** The credit note cannot be deleted without the permission to delete purchase credit notes, and the payment it draws on can no longer be deleted. Applying a purchase credit note records a purchase payment, and deleting a purchase credit note cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Every write body is wrapped in `purchase_credit_note`.** The note create is a multipart form instead.
- **The create takes `purchase_invoice_id` and `amount`.** The credit is drawn from that invoice's payments.
- **The apply, note and delete calls take the `pcn_` id only.** A code returns `400` there, while the reads accept either.
- **The create and apply responses are shorter than a read.** Their `status` is an object with `key` and `value`, where a read sends a string.
- **The delete and the note create need extra permissions.** A client without them gets `403`.
- **Listings page by cursor.** Records are under `data`; pass `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor` while `pagination.has_more` is `true`.
