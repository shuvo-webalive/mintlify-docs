Alerts flag records that need attention. You can list every alert, list the alerts of one account, employee or labour, and retrieve a single alert. This area has 5 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [List alerts](/api-reference/alerts/list-alerts) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/alerts` | Lists the alerts on your instance, one page at a time. |
| [Retrieve an alert](/api-reference/alerts/retrieve-an-alert) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/alerts/{alert_id}` | Retrieves one alert by its id. |
| [List an account's alerts](/api-reference/alerts/list-an-accounts-alerts) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/alerts/account/{account_id}` | Lists the alerts raised for one account. |
| [List an employee's alerts](/api-reference/alerts/list-an-employees-alerts) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/alerts/employee/{employee_id}` | Lists the alerts raised for one employee. |
| [List a labour's alerts](/api-reference/alerts/list-a-labours-alerts) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/alerts/labour/{labour_id}` | Lists the alerts raised for one labour. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Alerts are read only.** You can list them for the instance or for one account, employee or labour, and read one by id.
- **Employees and labours are identified by their `code`.** An account is named by its `id`, such as `W13ZGY`.
- **Every list has the same shape.** Alerts sit under `data` beside `object`, `url` and `pagination`, and an empty `data` means no alerts.
- **Listings page by cursor.** Records sit under `data`; pass `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor` to read the next page, and stop when `pagination.has_more` is `false`.
