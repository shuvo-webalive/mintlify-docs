An order is what an account buys: its lines, billing preferences, invoices and payments. This area creates and reads orders, quotes and contracts, changes their lines and billing, moves them through their states, and manages their notes and files. It has 60 endpoints, and every SDK supports all of them.

<Warning>
  **Only the preview endpoints are dry runs.** Every other state change, such as `upgrade`, `cancel` or `discard_draft`, takes effect at once. Discarding a draft removes it, so the order can no longer be read.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Most order bodies go under `order`.** The exceptions are `upgrade`, `downgrade`, `contract_adjustment` and their previews (`effective_date` and `lines` at the top level), the dormant invoice call (`activate`), the express call (`account`), and the note calls, which take a multipart form.
- **A contract needs a recurring or metered item on a line.** Set `allow_contract` to `true` and send `contract_properties` when you create the order.
- **Lines are named two ways.** Line paths take the line's `id` as `line_uuid`; a change body names a line by its `charge_item_uuid`, sent as `uuid`.
- **Previewing an order can still create an account.** `POST /orders/preview` creates no order, but a body that names a new account creates that account.
- **Payments and fulfillments are not checked against the order.** `GET /orders/{order_id}/payments/{payment_id}` and `.../item_fulfillments/{fulfillment_id}` read the record by its own `id`.
- **Listings page by cursor.** Send `limit`, then pass `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor` while `pagination.has_more` is `true`.
