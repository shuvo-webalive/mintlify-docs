Accounts are the customers and suppliers you bill and buy from. Here you create, read, change, cancel and delete accounts, manage their contacts, addresses, payment methods, billing preferences, notes, image and emails, and list the orders, invoices, payments and other records that belong to an account. This area has 65 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **Purging an account removes it and its records permanently, and cannot be undone.**
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Paths take the account's `id`, not its `uuid`.** The `id` is a short code such as `W13ZGY`; passing the `uuid` returns `404`.
- **Most writes wrap their fields in one key.** Account writes use `account`, a single address `address`, a contact `contact`, a payment method `payment_method` and billing preferences `billing_preferences`. Setting a password and sending an email take their fields at the top level, and adding addresses takes a top-level `addresses` list.
- **A contact is read by its slot and written by its id.** Retrieve a contact with its slot, `CONTACT_1` to `CONTACT_5`; update, replace and delete it with its `con_` id. A contact created as `CONTACT_1` reads back with `type` `billing`.
- **Writes are flatter than reads.** `currency` and `time_zone` go in as a code and a name and read back as objects, an address is written with `post_code` and reads back with `postal_code`, and a contact's name is written as `first_name` and `last_name` and reads back under `name`.
- **Many reads and every account write return the whole account.** The contacts, addresses, payment methods, billing preferences, inventories and partial invoice reads return the account under `account`, carrying the part asked for, rather than a paged listing.
- **Paged listings use a cursor.** Records come under `data` with a `pagination` object; while `has_more` is `true`, pass `pagination.next_cursor` as `cursor` to get the next page.
