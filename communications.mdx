Communications send documents by email. This area emails one invoice, purchase invoice, account statement or payment to the recipients you name. It has 1 endpoint, and all seven SDKs call it.

<Warning>
  **Every call sends real email to the recipients you name.**
</Warning>

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Email a document](/api-reference/communications/email-a-document) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#2563eb"}}>POST</span> | `…/communications/send_email` | Emails one invoice, purchase invoice, account statement or payment. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **The body names one document.** Send one of `invoice`, `purchase_invoice`, `account_statement` or `payment`, holding that document's `id`.
- **Recipients and content sit beside the document key.** The body also takes `to`, `cc`, `bcc`, `subject`, `body` and `template`.
- **Each call sends real mail.** Use addresses you control when you try it.
