A payment records money received against one or more invoices, by processor, credit note or gift certificate. This area has 15 endpoints: create, read, update and delete payments, list them by invoice or order, and manage their notes and files. All seven SDKs call every one.

<Warning>
  **Creating a payment with a `payment_applied` entry whose processor holds a card token charges the card.**
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Write bodies go under a `payment` key.** Creating a payment accepts only `id`, `date`, `origin`, `payment_note`, `note`, `invoices`, `payment_applied`, `credit_applied`, `gift_certificates` and `custom_attributes`; any other key returns `400`.
- **Listings page by cursor.** Records are under `data`. Pass `pagination.next_cursor` back as `cursor` while `pagination.has_more` is `true`.
- **Deleting a note takes its uuid, not its `id`.** Reads take the note's `id`; the delete takes the hex part of that `id` as a dashed uuid.
- **Reading a payment through an invoice does not check the invoice.** Any payment is returned under any invoice that exists.
- **Note text goes in a form field.** Creating a note sends a multipart form with the text as `note` and any attachment as `file`.
