Units of measurement are the settings records for units, each with a `type` such as `LENGTH`, a number of `decimal_places` and a `rounding` rule. These endpoints list, read, create, update and delete them. This area has 5 endpoints, and all seven SDKs call every one.

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **One unit comes back under `uom`.** The path uses `uom` for one unit and for many: `/settings/uom` lists them and `/settings/uom/{uom_id}` reads one.
- **Writes are wrapped in `uom`.** A create sends a `name` and a `type`, such as `LENGTH`.
- **`status` takes `active` or `inactive`.** Send it on the update to turn a unit on or off.
- **Shipping packages refer to a unit by its bare uuid.** A shipping package's `*_uom` fields take the unit's uuid, not its prefixed `id`.
- **The listing pages by cursor.** Pass `limit`, then send the `next_cursor` from `pagination` as `cursor` until `has_more` is `false`.
