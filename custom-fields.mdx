Custom fields are the extra fields set up in your Exsited settings. This area has 2 read-only endpoints, one to list the custom fields and one to read a single field, and all seven SDKs call every one.

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [List custom fields](/api-reference/custom-fields/list-custom-fields) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/settings/custom_fields` | Returns one page of the custom fields in your settings. |
| [Retrieve a custom field](/api-reference/custom-fields/retrieve-a-custom-field) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `/settings/custom_fields/{custom_field_id}` | Returns one custom field, under `custom_field`. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **This area is read only.** There are no create, update or delete calls for custom fields here.
- **A custom field id starts with `cfld_`.** Pass that `id` to read one field.
- **One field comes back under `custom_field`.** The listing puts its records under `data`.
- **The listing pages by cursor.** Pass `limit`, then the `next_cursor` from `pagination` as `cursor` while `has_more` is true.
