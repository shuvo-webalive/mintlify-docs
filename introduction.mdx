Exsited is an integration-first operations platform built for growing businesses that need more than a patchwork of disconnected tools. Whether you manage stock across multiple warehouses, process hundreds of sales orders a day, or need real-time sync with your accounting software, Exsited brings every part of your operations into a single, connected workspace — so your team always works from the same source of truth.

## Who Uses Exsited

Exsited is designed for businesses where operations span multiple systems, teams, or sales channels — and where manual data entry is costing time and accuracy.

- **Retailers & eCommerce merchants** use Exsited to sync orders and inventory across Shopify, Amazon, eBay, and BigCommerce, with accounting posted automatically to Xero or MYOB.
- **Wholesalers & distributors** rely on Exsited's purchasing and catalogue modules to manage supplier orders, landed costs, and multi-location stock.
- **Trade & field-service businesses** connect AroFlo and their accounting platform through Exsited to turn job completions into invoices without lifting a finger.
- **Compliance-driven businesses** use Exsited's audit trails, role-based access, and custom portal logic to maintain accurate records and controlled data flows.

If your team is stitching together spreadsheets, copy-pasting between systems, or losing visibility when orders move between departments, Exsited is built for you.
