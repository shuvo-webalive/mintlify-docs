The organisation endpoint reads the root organisation of your Exsited instance: its name, type, contact and address fields. The root organisation is read only. This area has 1 endpoint, and all seven SDKs call every one.

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Retrieve the root organisation](/api-reference/organisation/retrieve-the-root-organisation) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `…/organisation` | Returns the root organisation of your instance under `organization`. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **The response sits under `organization`, spelled with a z.**
- **The root organisation is read only.** A `PATCH` to `/settings/organisation` returns `405`.
- **The root's `type` is `root`, and it names itself as its `parent_organization`.**
- **Child organisations are a separate area.** They are listed under `/settings/organisations`, and the root is never among them.
