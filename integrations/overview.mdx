Exsited is built integration-first, meaning your existing tools — from your accounting package to your online store — connect directly into your operations workflow. Rather than re-entering data across systems, you configure once and let Exsited handle the movement of orders, invoices, inventory, customers, and more across your entire stack. Every integration is designed to be reliable, auditable, and configurable to match how your business actually works.

## How Integrations Work

Every Exsited integration follows the same core mechanics, giving you a consistent experience regardless of which platform you're connecting.

### Visual Field Mapping

When you connect a platform, Exsited presents a visual field mapping interface. This lets you drag and match fields between Exsited and the external system — for example, mapping your Exsited product SKU to the corresponding field in your accounting chart of accounts or your eCommerce platform's variant ID. You are not locked into defaults; every mapping is customisable.

### Real-Time vs Scheduled Sync

You choose how frequently data moves between systems:

- **Real-time sync** — data is pushed or pulled immediately when a trigger event occurs (for example, a new order placed or an invoice marked paid). Ideal for inventory and order management where delays cause overselling or fulfilment errors.
- **Scheduled sync** — data syncs at a defined interval (for example, every 15 minutes, hourly, or nightly). Well-suited for accounting updates where immediate sync is less critical.

### Sync Logs and Conflict Handling

Every sync event is recorded in the Exsited sync log. You can review what was synced, when, and whether any errors occurred. When conflicts arise — for example, a record updated in both systems before a sync — Exsited surfaces the conflict and lets you choose the resolution rule: last-write-wins, Exsited-first, or external-system-first.

## Connecting Any Platform

All integrations follow the same connection pattern in Exsited:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Navigate to **Integrations** in your Exsited dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Platform">
    Choose the platform you want to connect from the list. If you don't see it, contact [support@exsited.com](mailto:support@exsited.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorise the Connection">
    Follow the on-screen prompts to authorise Exsited to access your platform — typically via OAuth or API key entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Field Mappings">
    Use the visual field mapper to match Exsited fields to the corresponding fields in the external system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Sync Settings">
    Select real-time or scheduled sync, set conflict resolution rules, and enable the integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Custom Integrations via Webhooks

If your platform is not listed, Exsited supports outbound webhooks for custom integrations. You can configure a webhook endpoint to receive real-time event payloads — such as order created, invoice updated, or stock level changed — and handle them in your own middleware or iPaaS tool (for example, Zapier, Make, or a custom API).

To set up a webhook, see the [Webhooks guide](/api/webhooks) in the Developer documentation.
