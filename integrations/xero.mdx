Connecting Exsited to Xero eliminates the manual work of re-entering financial data between your operations platform and your accounting package. Once configured, Exsited pushes invoices, payments, credit notes, and contacts directly into Xero — and pulls back tax codes and chart-of-accounts structures to keep your field mappings accurate. The integration uses OAuth 2.0, so your Xero credentials are never stored in Exsited.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have the following:

- An active Xero organisation with at least **Standard** or **Adviser** user access.
- An Exsited account with the **Admin** role. Operator-level users cannot connect or modify integrations.
- Your Xero chart of accounts (COA) set up with the account codes you want to map to Exsited.

## Connecting Xero to Exsited

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Xero integration">
    In your Exsited dashboard, go to **Integrations → Accounting → Xero**. If this is your first accounting integration, you will see a prompt to connect a platform. Click **Connect Xero**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorise via OAuth">
    Exsited will redirect you to the Xero authorisation screen. Log in with your Xero credentials and select the Xero organisation you want to connect. Click **Allow access** to grant Exsited the required permissions. You will be redirected back to Exsited once authorisation is complete.

    <Note>
      Exsited requests read and write access to your Xero accounting data. No access to payroll or practice manager data is requested.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your chart of accounts">
    Once connected, Exsited imports your Xero account codes. Use the visual field mapping screen to match your Exsited revenue categories, expense categories, and tax codes to the corresponding Xero accounts. At minimum, map:

    - **Sales account** — the Xero revenue account for customer invoices.
    - **Accounts receivable account** — the debtor control account.
    - **Tax codes** — map each Exsited tax code (for example, GST, GST-free, BAS excluded) to the equivalent Xero tax rate.

    <Tip>
      If you use multiple revenue streams (for example, product sales and service fees), create separate Exsited categories and map each to its own Xero account for cleaner reporting.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure sync settings">
    Choose your sync behaviour:

    - **Sync direction** — Exsited → Xero only, or bidirectional (for contacts and payments).
    - **Sync frequency** — real-time (recommended for invoices and payments) or scheduled.
    - **Invoice status trigger** — choose which Exsited invoice status pushes the record to Xero (for example, **Approved** or **Sent**).
    - **Multi-currency** — if your Xero organisation has multi-currency enabled, select whether to sync the original transaction currency or convert to your base currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Click **Test Connection** to send a test invoice to Xero in draft status. Open your Xero dashboard and confirm the draft invoice appears with the correct account codes, tax rates, and line items. Once verified, delete the test invoice from Xero and click **Activate** in Exsited to enable live syncing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Syncs

| Data Type | Direction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Customer invoices | Exsited → Xero | Pushed when invoice reaches configured trigger status |
| Payments | Exsited → Xero | Matched to the corresponding Xero invoice |
| Credit notes | Exsited → Xero | Linked to original invoice where applicable |
| Purchase invoices | Exsited → Xero | Supplier bills pushed from Exsited purchase orders |
| Contacts (customers & suppliers) | Bidirectional | New contacts synced both ways; updates follow conflict rules |
| Tax codes | Xero → Exsited | Pulled during initial setup and refreshed on demand |

## Field Mapping Guidance

Exsited's visual mapper shows Exsited fields on the left and Xero fields on the right. Connect them by clicking a field and selecting its counterpart. Key mappings to review:

- **Reference number** — map Exsited invoice number to Xero invoice reference to avoid duplicate detection issues.
- **Due date** — map Exsited payment terms to Xero due date. If you use dynamic terms (for example, Net 30), Exsited calculates the due date at sync time.
- **Line item description** — choose whether to sync the Exsited product name, SKU, or a custom description field.
- **GST/VAT treatment** — confirm each line item tax code maps to a Xero tax rate with the correct tax type (for example, OUTPUT2 for 10% GST on income).

<Tip>
  Use the **Preview** button in the field mapper to see a sample record rendered with your current mappings before activating the integration.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Authorisation expired — sync is failing">
    Xero OAuth tokens expire after 60 days of inactivity. If your sync stops working, navigate to **Integrations → Accounting → Xero** and click **Reconnect**. You will be taken through the OAuth flow again. No field mappings or sync settings are lost during reconnection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Duplicate invoice number error">
    Xero rejects invoices with a reference number that already exists in the organisation. This usually happens if an invoice was created manually in Xero before the integration was set up. To resolve this, either delete the duplicate in Xero or change the Exsited invoice number prefix under **Settings → Invoicing → Numbering** so that Exsited-generated numbers do not clash with existing ones.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tax code not found in Xero">
    If Exsited cannot find a matching tax rate in Xero, the sync will fail with a "tax rate not found" error. Go to **Integrations → Accounting → Xero → Field Mappings → Tax Codes** and reassign any unmapped Exsited tax codes to a valid Xero tax rate. Then click **Retry Failed Records** to re-push the affected invoices.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Contact records creating duplicates in Xero">
    Exsited matches contacts by email address. If a contact in Xero does not have an email address, or the email differs from the one in Exsited, a new contact will be created. Ensure email addresses are consistent across both platforms, or disable bidirectional contact sync and manage contacts from a single source.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  If you encounter an error not listed above, check the **Sync Log** under **Integrations → Accounting → Xero → Logs** for the full error message. You can also contact [support@exsited.com](mailto:support@exsited.com) with the log entry reference.
</Note>
