Purchase express creates a purchase order for a supplier in one call. The API also accepts a nested purchase invoice and payment, but their fields are not documented yet. This area has 1 endpoint, available in all seven SDKs.

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Create an express purchase order](/api-reference/purchase-express/create-an-express-purchase-order) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#2563eb"}}>POST</span> | `/purchase_express` | Creates a purchase order for a supplier in one call and returns it under `purchase_express`. |

## Before you call

Register your API credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **The body is wrapped in `account`.** The supplier goes in `account.id` and the order in `account.purchase_order`.
- **Without a nested purchase invoice, nothing is billed or paid.** Only the purchase order is created.
- **The new purchase order comes back under `purchase_express`.** Read or manage it afterwards through the purchase order endpoints.
