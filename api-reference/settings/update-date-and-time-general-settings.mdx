---
title: "Update date and time general settings"
description: "Changes settings in the `date_and_time` group of general settings and returns the group under `general_settings`."
openapi: "PATCH /settings/general_settings/date_and_time"
---

Send the settings to change under `general_settings.date_and_time`. The group holds `date_format`, `time_format`, `time_zone` and `first_day_of_week`. Saving this group also sends the organisation to the catalogue processor, an external call.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token). To send a raw JSON body instead of filling in fields, copy the cURL sample and edit its `-d` payload.</Note>
