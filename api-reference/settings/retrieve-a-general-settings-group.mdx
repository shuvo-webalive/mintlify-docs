---
title: "Retrieve a general settings group"
description: "Returns one group of general settings, keyed by the group, under `general_settings`."
openapi: "GET /settings/general_settings/{group}"
---

Reads one group of general settings. `group` is one of `account`, `item`, `date_and_time`, `order`, `purchase`, `labour`, `transaction`, `security`, `dashboard`, `portal`, `display` or `general`, in any letter case. The response holds the group under `general_settings.<group>`, and the `item` and `order` groups also carry `product`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
