---
title: "Retrieve a purchase order's information"
description: "Returns the header information of one purchase order under `purchase_order`."
openapi: "GET /purchase_orders/{purchase_order_id}/information"
---

Returns the purchase order's header fields, such as its dates, totals, status and custom attributes, under `purchase_order`, without the supplier, currency and KPI blocks.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
