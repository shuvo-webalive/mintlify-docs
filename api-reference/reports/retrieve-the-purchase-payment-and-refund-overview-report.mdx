---
title: "Retrieve the purchase payment and refund overview report"
description: "Returns the purchase payments and purchase refunds made and deleted for each dated entry in a period."
openapi: "GET /reports/purchase_transactions/purchase_payment_and_refund_overview"
---

The report is under `report.purchase_payment_and_refund_overview`, with a `purchase_payments` list and a `purchase_refunds` list. Each entry has a `date` and an `amount` and `count` for `new` and `deleted`; a purchase payment entry also has `purchase_payment_applied`. Choose the period with `date_range`, or `custom` with `from` and `to`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
