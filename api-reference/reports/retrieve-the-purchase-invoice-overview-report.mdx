---
title: "Retrieve the purchase invoice overview report"
description: "Returns purchase invoice totals and the amount and count of new, cancelled, reactivated, voided and amended purchase invoices for each dated entry in a period."
openapi: "GET /reports/purchase_invoices/purchase_invoice_overview"
---

The report is under `report.purchase_invoices.purchase_invoice_overview`. Each entry has a `date`, a `purchase_invoice_total`, and an `amount` and `count` for `new`, `cancelled`, `reactivated`, `voided`, `amended` and `net_gain`. Choose the period with `date_range`, or `custom` with `from` and `to`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
