---
title: "Create a purchase credit note"
description: "Credits a purchase invoice and returns the new credit note under `purchase_credit_note`."
openapi: "POST /purchase_credit_notes"
---

Creates a purchase credit note against a purchase invoice, wrapped in `purchase_credit_note` with the invoice's `purchase_invoice_id` and the `amount`. The credit is drawn from the invoice's payments. The response is shorter than a read, and its `status` is an object with `key` and `value`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token). To send a raw JSON body instead of filling in fields, copy the cURL sample and edit its `-d` payload.</Note>
