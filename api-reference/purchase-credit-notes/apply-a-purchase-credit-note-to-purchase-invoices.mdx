---
title: "Apply a purchase credit note to purchase invoices"
description: "Spends a purchase credit note on one or more purchase invoices and returns the credit note under `purchase_credit_note`."
openapi: "POST /purchase_credit_notes/{purchase_credit_note_id}/apply"
---

Applies the credit to purchase invoices, listed under `purchase_credit_note.purchase_invoices`. Each entry takes `purchase_invoice_id` and `amount` and nothing else. Applying the credit records a purchase payment. Address the credit note by its `pcn_` id.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token). To send a raw JSON body instead of filling in fields, copy the cURL sample and edit its `-d` payload.</Note>
