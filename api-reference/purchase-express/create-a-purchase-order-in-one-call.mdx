---
title: "Create a purchase order in one call"
description: "Creates a purchase order, and optionally its purchase invoice and payment, and returns the order under `purchase_express`."
openapi: "POST /purchase_express"
---

Creates a purchase order for a supplier in one call, with the body under `account.purchase_order`. If the body also nests a purchase invoice and payment, they are created in the same call; without them, nothing is billed or paid. The new purchase order comes back under `purchase_express`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token). To send a raw JSON body instead of filling in fields, copy the cURL sample and edit its `-d` payload.</Note>
