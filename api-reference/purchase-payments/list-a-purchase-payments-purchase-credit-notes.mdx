---
title: "List a purchase payment's purchase credit notes"
description: "Returns one page of the purchase credit notes a purchase payment raised."
openapi: "GET /purchase_payments/{purchase_payment_id}/purchase_credit_notes"
---

Lists the purchase credit notes the payment raised, under `data`. A payment raises one when it pays more than its invoices take; a payment that does not overpay has an empty list.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
