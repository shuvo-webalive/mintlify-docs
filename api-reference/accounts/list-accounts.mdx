---
title: "List accounts"
description: "Lists your accounts a page at a time, with optional filters and sorting."
openapi: "GET /accounts"
---

Returns accounts under `data`, with `pagination` for the next page. Narrow the listing with `filter_<field>` for equality or `filter_<field>_<operator>` for other tests, such as `filter_created_on_gte` or `filter_email_address_contains`. Every filterable field also takes `_neq`; the text fields take `_contains` and `_not_contains`, and `created_on` and `last_updated_on` take `_gt`, `_gte`, `_lt` and `_lte`. The primary contact filters as `filter_contact1_<field>` and a custom attribute as `filter_attribute_<name>`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
