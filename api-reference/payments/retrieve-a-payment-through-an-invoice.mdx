---
title: "Retrieve a payment through an invoice"
description: "Returns one payment by its `id`, reached through an invoice."
openapi: "GET /invoices/{invoice_id}/payments/{payment_id}"
---

Fetches a payment under `payment`. The server checks only that the invoice exists, not that the payment belongs to it. Each entry of the payment's `invoices` names its invoice by the invoice's `inv_` uuid, not by its `id`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
