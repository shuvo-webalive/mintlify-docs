---
title: "Create a purchase refund from a purchase credit note"
description: "Records a refund from a supplier against a purchase credit note and returns it under `purchase_refund`."
openapi: "POST /purchase_credit_notes/{purchase_credit_note_id}/purchase_refunds"
---

Records a refund against the purchase credit note in the path, wrapped in `purchase_refund`. `payment_method` is the reference of one of the supplier's payment methods. A purchase refund is always raised against a purchase credit note; there is no create on the purchase refunds path.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token). To send a raw JSON body instead of filling in fields, copy the cURL sample and edit its `-d` payload.</Note>
