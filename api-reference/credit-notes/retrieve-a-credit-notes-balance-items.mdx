---
title: "Retrieve a credit note's balance items"
description: "Returns what a credit note has credited and refunded, by source."
openapi: "GET /credit_notes/{credit_note_id}/items"
---

Fetches the credit note's balance rows under `credit_note.items`. Each row splits the credit and refund totals by source, such as payment method, gift certificate or invoice credit, each amount a string. This is not a paged listing.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
