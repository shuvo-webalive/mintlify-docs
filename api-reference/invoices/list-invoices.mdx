---
title: "List invoices"
description: "Returns one page of invoices, with a cursor for the next page."
openapi: "GET /invoices"
---

Lists every invoice you can see, one page at a time. The records are under `data` and the paging details under `pagination`. A listed invoice does not include its `lines`; retrieve the invoice to get them. Other fields filter as `filter_<field>`, or `filter_<field>_<operator>` with an operator such as `gte` or `contains`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
