---
title: "List a purchase invoice's lines"
description: "Returns the lines of a purchase invoice under `purchase_invoice.lines`."
openapi: "GET /purchase_invoices/{purchase_invoice_id}/lines"
---

Returns the purchase invoice's lines as `purchase_invoice.lines`, not as a paged `data` list. This read is not paged, and `limit`, `cursor` and `sort` are not documented to change it. Use a line's `id` to read it on its own.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
