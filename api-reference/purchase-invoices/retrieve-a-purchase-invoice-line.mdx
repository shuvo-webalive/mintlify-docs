---
title: "Retrieve a purchase invoice line"
description: "Returns one line of a purchase invoice under `purchase_invoice.line`."
openapi: "GET /purchase_invoices/{purchase_invoice_id}/lines/{purchase_invoice_line_uuid}"
---

Returns a single line of the purchase invoice under `purchase_invoice.line`. Address the line by the `id` the lines list shows, or by that `id` written as a UUID.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `host` to your Exsited API host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
